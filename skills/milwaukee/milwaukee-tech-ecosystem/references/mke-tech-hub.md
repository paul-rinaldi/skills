# MKE Tech Hub Coalition

Nonprofit coalition growing Milwaukee's tech talent and ecosystem: https://mketech.org. Program pages move around, so search mketech.org for current details and dates. For contacts, use the site's contact page rather than staff emails.

## Programs
| Program | Who it's for | Link |
|---|---|---|
| FOR-M | First-time tech startup founders getting plugged into the ecosystem | https://www.mketech.org/for-m |
| Synapse | Leaders navigating AI and emerging tech | https://mketech.org |
| AI Roadmap Sprint for Startups | Startups planning AI adoption | https://mketech.org |
| AI Workshops for Manufacturers | Manufacturers | https://mketech.org |
| Catalyze | Tech leaders turning technical talent into enterprise momentum (Workforce team) | https://mketech.org |
| FUSE | College students building technical and AI skills (2-week sprints) | https://mketech.org |
| AI Community | AI professionals and enthusiasts | https://www.mketech.org/ai/community/ |

## Recurring events
- **Founders Day** (monthly): founders and mentors
- **Welcome to MKE Tech**: socials for people new to Milwaukee tech jobs
- **AI Quarterly**: AI Community gathering
- **Community Tech Event Calendar**: on mketech.org

## Getting involved
- **Membership**: contact the Engagement team via mketech.org
- **Job board**: post or browse via mketech.org (Marketing team handles postings)
- **Newsletter**: twice monthly; sign up on mketech.org
- **Volunteering**: one-hour virtual commitments coaching FUSE students or FOR-M founders, through MKE Tech's interest form

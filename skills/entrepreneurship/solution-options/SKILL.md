# Solution Options

Source method: FOR-M Workshop 2. Creativity means creating choices. There are three places to get creative: **product features**, **business model**, and **prototype**. For the prototype, hand off to mvp-experiment-designer. This skill covers the first two.

## Part A: Product features, layer by layer
Generate at least 2 options per layer, then mark which ones are essential for a first version:
1. Core functionality: the one thing it must do
2. Added-value functionality
3. Technical backend
4. Visual frontend
5. User experience
6. Brand
7. Marketing

## Part B: Business model (not a financial model)
- **Value imbalance.** Separate the value created from the value captured. List hard savings (money, time) and soft savings (stress, risk, reputation).
- **Customers, users, and multi-sided markets.** Answer for each party:
  - Who benefits?
  - Where's the money?
  - Who would pay, and how?

  The user and the payer are often different people, for example job seekers and employers or agencies.
- **You'll want options.** Draft at least 3 distinct models, such as subscription, per-placement fee, grant- or agency-funded, or freemium. Give the strongest risk for each.

## Output
Deliver these:
- A feature table: each layer, its options, and the v1 pick
- 3 business model sketches, each with who benefits, who pays, and how
- A recommended combination with a one-line rationale, while keeping the alternates visible

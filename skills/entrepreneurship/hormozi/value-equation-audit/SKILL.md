# Value Equation Audit

Based on Alex Hormozi's *$100M Offers*. Perceived value goes **up** with the dream outcome and the buyer's belief that they'll get it. It goes **down** with how long it takes and how much effort or sacrifice it costs them.

> Value ≈ (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

## Step 1: Get the offer down
Ask for or extract the target customer, what they get, the price, how it's delivered, and the most common objections. If there is no clear customer yet, run **market-check** first.

## Step 2: Score each lever (1–5, from the buyer's point of view)
| Lever | Ask | Good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Is the result something they deeply want, in their own words? | A vivid, status-raising end state, not a feature list |
| Perceived likelihood | Do they believe it will work *for them*? | Proof, testimonials, a guarantee, a credible mechanism |
| Time delay | How long until they feel a result? | A quick early win, even if the full result takes longer |
| Effort & sacrifice | What do they have to do or give up? | Done-for-you parts, fewer steps, nothing they hate doing |

The bottom two count as much as the top two. Most offers over-invest in the dream outcome and ignore time and effort.

## Step 3: Diagnose
- Name the **weakest lever** and the **cheapest lever to improve**. These are often different, so recommend one.
- For each of the four levers, suggest 1–2 concrete changes, such as adding a fast-start session, removing a step, or collecting proof.
- Flag any lever where the score is a guess, and say how to check it, for example by asking 5 customers.

## Output
- The score table with a one-line reason per lever
- The top 3 changes, ranked by impact divided by cost
- A rewrite of the offer's one-sentence promise that reflects those changes

To rebuild the offer from scratch, hand off to **grand-slam-offer-builder**. To add bonuses, guarantees, and urgency, use **offer-enhancers**.

# Offer Enhancers

Based on Alex Hormozi's *$100M Offers*. Once the core offer is built (see **grand-slam-offer-builder**), five enhancers increase demand. Use them honestly. Fake scarcity or deadlines destroy trust.

## 1. Scarcity (limited supply)
Choose a real limit and explain why it exists: seats per cohort, clients per month, or a limited batch. Say how the limit will be shown publicly.

## 2. Urgency (limited time)
Choose a real time trigger: a cohort start date, seasonal timing, a price going up, or a bonus that expires. Give each trigger a real date or reason.

## 3. Bonuses
- Each bonus should solve one obstacle the buyer will hit next, ideally one left over from the obstacle list.
- Give each bonus a name, a stated value, and a one-line reason it matters to *them*.
- Several specific bonuses beat one big vague one.
- When possible, use bonuses from partners: another business supplies a tool or service at no cost to you.

## 4. Guarantee
Pick the type that fits the risk, and write it in plain language:
- **Unconditional:** refund with no questions asked
- **Conditional:** refund (or more) if they did the work and didn't get the result
- **Anti-guarantee:** all sales final, with a stated reason (for example, instant access to something that can't be returned)
- **Implied or performance-based:** they pay only when results happen, or pay a percentage of results

Note the downside risk to the business and any conditions that make the guarantee safe to offer.

## 5. Naming
Build the name from these ingredients, then draft 5 options:
- Who it's for
- The result
- A timeframe
- A container word, such as *program*, *sprint*, *system*, or *challenge*

Keep it short, specific, and about *them*. Pick one and explain why.

## Output
For each enhancer, give a recommendation, the exact wording to use, and any honesty check (such as "only claim this if the cap is real"). End with the full offer as it would appear on a sales page: name, promise, stack, bonuses, guarantee, and urgency or scarcity line.
